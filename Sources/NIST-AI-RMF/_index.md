# NIST AI RMF Sources

> Source files for the NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework.

## Files

- [[nist-ai-rmf-1-0]] — Complete AI RMF 1.0 publication (48 pages) covering Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions with all categories and subcategories

## Functions Overview

| Function | Categories | Description |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| GOVERN | GV-1 through GV-6 | Policies, accountability, workforce diversity, culture, engagement, third-party management |
| MAP | MP-1 through MP-5 | Context, categorization, capabilities/goals/benefits, component risks, impact characterization |
| MEASURE | MS-1 through MS-4 | Methods/metrics, trustworthiness evaluation, risk tracking, feedback |
| MANAGE | MG-1 through MG-4 | Risk prioritization/response, benefit maximization, third-party management, risk treatment plans |
