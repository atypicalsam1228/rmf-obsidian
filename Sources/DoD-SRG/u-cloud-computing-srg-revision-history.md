## Revision History
Document Revised Description of Change Release Date
- Cloud Service Provider Cloud Service Provider SRG, V1R6: 10 December
SRG, V1R5 - Made minor updates to wording in 5.2.2.3 to 2025
clarify Impact Level 5 separation requirements.
- Updated Section 5.2.2.6 to clarify the scope is
Virtual Machine (VM) Isolation and Integrity.
Moved the VDI VM bullets to the Mission
Owner STIG Overview.
- Updated Section 5.2.4.2 to clarify crypto erase
requirements.
- In Section 5.9.1.1.1, added that missions that
handle CUI (i.e., Impact Level 4/5 applications)
supporting internet-based users rather than
NIPRNet-based users can be protected by an
approved CNAP.
- Updated Section 5.9.3.1 to ensure that
providing NIPRNet access to internet-facing
services does not grant default internet access
to all tenants.
- Made minor clarification to wording in
Section 5.17 and subsections pertaining to
remote maintenance support.
- Cloud Service Provider Cloud Service Provider SRG, V1R5: 03 September
SRG, V1R4 - Added bullets to Section 5.2.2.4, IL6 Location 2025
and Separation Requirements.
- Revised Section 5.2.4.1, Encryption of Data at
Rest and In Transit in Commercial Cloud
Storage.
- Noted in Section 5.8, Reuse and Disposal of
Storage Media and Hardware, that any reuse of
equipment to a non-DOD tenant requires AO
approval.
- Added a paragraph to Section 5.9.4, IP
Addressing and DNS, about RFC 1918 IP
addresses.
- Added Section 5.9.5.4, Cross Domain
Solutions.
- Revised Section 5.17, Remote Maintenance
and Support, to address logging requirements.
- Revised Section 5.17.3, Restrictions on
Escorted Access.
- Clarified bulleted text in Section 6.2, Cyber
Incident Reporting and Response.
- Cloud Service Provider Cloud Service Provider SRG, V1R4: 13 August 2025
