| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Continuous Monitoring Monthly Executive Summary | CA-05, RA-05a | An executive summary of the monthly continuous monitoring (ConMon) submission that includes the most current Plan Of Actions and Milestones (POA&M), submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Monthly |
| Collaborative Continuous Monitoring | CA-07g | CSOs with more than one (1) agency ATO must implement a collaborative ConMon approach as described in the FedRAMP Collaborative ConMon Quick Guide, a guide for Multi-Agency Continuous Monitoring. This requirement applies to all FedRAMP-authorized CSOs, as each agency customer is responsible for performing ConMon oversight. | CSP defined, in accordance with the FedRAMP Collaborative ConMon Quick Guide (https://www.fedramp.gov/documents-templates/) |
| Vulnerability and Configuration Scanning | RA-05a, CA-07, CM-06 | Operating System (OS)/infrastructure, web application, database, container and configuration scans submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Monthly |
| Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) | CA-05 | Updated as needed and submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Monthly |
| Inventory | CM-08 | Updated as needed and submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Monthly |
| Contingency Plan Test Plan | CP-04a | Documented plan to perform a test and exercise of the IT Contingency Plan; the plan is submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Moderate and High - Functional, at least annually <br>  <br> Low - Classroom exercise/table top written test, at least every 3 years <br>  <br> Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Contingency Plan Test Results | CP-04a | Perform contingency plan testing; the results are inserted into Appendix F of the IT Contingency Plan and submitted to the AO via the designated repository. Test date, results, and participants must be noted in the SSP. | Moderate and High - Functional, at least annually <br>  <br> Low - Classroom exercise/table top written test, at least every 3 years <br>  <br> Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Incident Response Test Plan | IR-03 | Documented plan to perform incident response testing is submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Moderate - Functional, at least annually <br>  <br> High - At least every six (6) months, including functional at least annually <br>  <br> Low and Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Incident Response Test Results | IR-03 | Perform incident response testing and submit to the AO via the designated repository. | Moderate - Functional, at least annually <br>  <br> High - At least every six (6) months, including functional at least annually <br>  <br> Low and Li-SaaS - N/A |
| System Security Plan (SSP) and Supporting Documents Update | PL-02c, PL-02d | Update the SSP and supporting documents, and submit to the 3PAO at least thirty (30) days prior to the initiation of annual assessment activities. | Annually |
| Static Code Analysis Methodology | SA-11 (01) | The service provider must document its methodology for reviewing newly developed code for the service in its ConMon plan. As with all SSP supporting documents, the ConMon plan must be submitted to the 3PAO at least thirty (30) days prior to the initiation of annual assessment activities. | Moderate and High - Annually <br>  <br> Low and Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Security Assessments Plan (SAP) | CA-02 | Plan for the annual assessment developed by the 3PAO and submitted to the AO via the designated repository. | Annually |
| Penetration Testing performed by Independent Assessor (High and Moderate) | CA-08, CA-08 (01) | Penetration test. For High and Moderate systems, penetration testing is conducted by the 3PAO and included in/with the SAR. Submit to the AO via the designated repository. | Annually |
| Penetration Testing (Low and Li-SaaS) | CA-08 | Penetration test. Submit the report to the AO via the designated repository. | Annually |
| Penetration Testing - Red Team Test Plan | CA-08 (02) | Documented plan to perform red team exercises. Submit the report to the AO via the designated repository. | Moderate and High - Annually <br>  <br> Low and Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Penetration Testing - Red Team Test Report | CA-08 (02) | Perform red team exercises and document the results. Submit the report to the AO via the designated repository. | Moderate and High - Annually <br>  <br> Low and Li-SaaS - N/A |
| Vulnerability and Configuration Scanning | RA-05a, CA-07, CM-06 | Scan of OS/infrastructure, web applications, containers, databases, and configurations conducted directly by the 3PAO or with 3PAO oversight and submitted in/with SAR. | Annually |
| Security Assessments and Security Assessment Report (SAR) | CA-02c | Security assessment, including any specialized assessments, conducted by the 3PAO. Submit the report to the AO via the designated repository. | Annually |
| Security Alerts, Advisories, and Directives | SI-05 | Service Providers must address the CISA Emergency and Binding Operational Directives applicable to their cloud service offering per FedRAMP guidance.  This includes listing the applicable directives and stating compliance status. | Ad hoc (in accordance with FedRAMP guidance) |
